I’m Sorry

said softly, honestly, without expectation
🌹

I want to begin with this — I am truly sorry for the way I spoke to you.

My tone wasn’t fair. It didn’t reflect the respect I have for you. I spoke from anxiety instead of understanding, and that’s something I take full responsibility for.

I’ve taken time to reflect, and I understand now that I let my fear shape my words and reactions. That fear came from emotional attachment — but that was never yours to handle.

You asking for space was reasonable. Especially during exams. I should have honored that immediately, without questions or pressure.

I also want to say something important.

You are genuinely a good person. Kind, honest, and thoughtful in your own way.

I’ve always appreciated the things you shared with me — your drawings, your art, the effort and emotion you put into them.

The way you see things and express yourself through your art is beautiful. It shows who you are, and I’ve always respected that.

And yes, you are beautiful — not just in your photos or your smile, but in the way you carry yourself and the way you treat people.

I’m grateful for the conversations we’ve had, for the trust you shared with me, and for the moments you chose to let me see your world.

This isn’t to make you feel bad, and it’s not to rush you into talking again.

I just wanted you to know that I truly value you and I’m genuinely sorry.

This isn’t an attempt to explain myself away, or to change how you feel, or to ask anything from you.

It’s simply an apology — for crossing a boundary and for not being as calm and respectful as I should have been.

I genuinely regret it.

🌸
🌹

Please take all the time and space you need. I respect it — completely.
